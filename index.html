Neo is an AI-powered architectural design and 3D automation platform under development, aiming to make advanced design and visualization workflows more intelligent, accessible, and efficient.

What We're Building

Architectural Intelligence

AI-assisted spatial planning, architectural concepts, and interior design workflows.

Professional 3D Creation

Parametric geometry, detailed modeling, material workflows, and scene automation.

Intelligent Visualization

Automated lighting, camera planning, rendering, and visual quality evaluation.

AI Research & Development

Exploring large language models, knowledge retrieval, and GPU-powered design intelligence.

Our Vision

We envision a future where complex architectural production workflows can be directed through natural language, while intelligent systems assist with modeling, technical decisions, and visualization.

Current Status: Neo is an independently developed, pre-incorporation project based in Türkiye. The platform is in active research, development, and technical validation. It is not yet commercially launched.
